# Manna Brand Design — "Verdant Vault"

## Direction
Modern fintech, dark & premium. Wise-inspired boldness adapted for B2B European savings.

## Symbol
3-4 soft rounded shapes descending diagonally — manna falling from the sky. Varying sizes (smaller at top, larger at bottom = accumulation). No container. Pure, open, elegant. Emerald on dark, midnight on light.

## Wordmark
"manna" lowercase, Outfit Medium (500), letter-spacing 0.15em.

## Lockups
- Horizontal: symbol left, wordmark right
- Stacked: symbol above, wordmark below
- Symbol-only: favicon, app icons, tight spaces

## Typography
- **Font**: Outfit (Google Fonts, variable)
- **Weights**: 400 body, 500 medium/labels, 600 headings/amounts, 700 hero
- **Wordmark**: 500, 0.15em spacing, lowercase
- **Financial figures**: 600, tabular-nums
- **Card numbers**: monospace (keep existing)
- **Loading**: next/font/google
